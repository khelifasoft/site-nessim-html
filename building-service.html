Building service

With years of experience in professional building cleaning in the private and commercial sector, we offer you reliable and high-quality work. Through our large network of specialist consultants, we guarantee competent cleaning services with high quality and modern customer service. The professional contact with customers, the staff and a pleasant working atmosphere is of great importance. Our cleaning and service personnel are trained at regular intervals and paid according to the building collective agreement of the building service providers. To be able to guarantee occupational safety at all times, the staff is instructed in occupational safety, the use of environmentally friendly cleaning products and rules of conduct in dealing with customers.

Our building service covers the following fields of work:

  • glass cleaning
  • maintenance cleaning
  • facade cleaning
  • basic cleaning
  • etc.

Professional. Individual. Safe.

Infection protection

Infectious diseases affect our lives and our businesses

  • COVID-19 (SARS-CoV-2)
  • Avian influenza (Influenza A virus H5N1)
  • Swine flu (Influenza A virus H1N1)
  • And others...

&feature=youtu.be

In order to minimise the adverse effects of these diseases, we must be aware of how and where they can be transmitted. Only then can we develop and implement measures to protect you and ourselves. No matter what problems or infections you are currently struggling with - we will work with you and overcome them.

Profis Industrie Service GmbH carries out various measures to protect the customer against infection.

  • Awareness raising and training of employees
    (distance rules and protective clothing)
  • Holiday control
    (compliance with quarantine regulations and risk areas)
  • Test phases on farms
    (prevention of infection and spread)
  • Work online
    (document processing and controlling using modern technology)
  • Temperature control devices
    (keep daily records)
  • Break regulation
    (separate areas and different times)
  • Avoid narrow working areas
    (controlled structure of workstations)
  • Regular cleaning & disinfection of workplaces
    And further...

We also offer a special cleaning and disinfection service to protect our customers against infection. This reduces viruses and germs to a minimum and keeps the risk of infection as low as possible.

  • Surface cleaning and disinfection (offices, supermarkets, ...) with P3-Alcodes & MaxiWipes
  • Disinfection for hands by P3-manodes LI especially against viruses
  • Disinfecting soap P3-Manosan against viruses and bacteria
  • Fogging/gassing of closed areas for disinfection of the room air
  • Fogging/gassing of closed areas for disinfection of the room air

We also have the suitable solution for your company.

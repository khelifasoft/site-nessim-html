ELA - Employee leasing approval

We lend our German employees for the cleaning service. Due to our flexibility, we do not only service companies in the nearer surroundings, but also all over Germany. Our employees are thus also distributed throughout Germany and are available for the cleaning service at any time and above all as quickly as possible.

Occupational health and safety protection are a high priority for management. Among our highest corporate goals are the strong relationship with our customers, the fulfillment of their special requirements and their satisfaction. Low costs for you and structured work steps for us.

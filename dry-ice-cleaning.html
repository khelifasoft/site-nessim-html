Dry ice cleaning

Home›Dry ice cleaning

Dry ice cleaning is a process that uses liquids other than water to clean. For many industries, this method is an effective and cost-effective method of maximizing production capability and quality as well as environmentally friendly cleaning. With the help of dry ice cleaning, we are able to clean the inner workings, but also stubborn dirt on surfaces.

The advantages of dry ice cleaning

Using this way cleaning without preparation
Machines do not have to be laboriously dismantled for cleaning. The pellets can easily reach even the smallest of corners. The dry ice pellets dissolve completely in CO² after blasting and basically do not cause any damage to electrical parts

No Residues
No residues Dry ice sublimes completely to CO2. There are no residues such as abrasives or waste water

Environmentally friendly
Cleaning without additional chemicals or abrasives

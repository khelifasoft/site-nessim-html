Statistics

Home›Statistics

More than just data.

Statistics have many important benefits. They enable us to react to errors,
take preventive measures, eliminate sources of problems and even strengthen our competitive position.

We produce various statistics, such as ...

cost statistics

personnel statistics

hygiene sample statistics

claim statistics

consumption statistics

Connecting means learning

The different statistics can be related to each other, opening up new perspectives.

Example: Recurring complaints about the quality of work, resulting from this:

more claims

rising costs

low customer satisfaction

The problems are analysed - by means of various guided statistics the problem can be quickly identified. By means of personnel statistics it can be seen that at the time of the complaints the same team was always working.

The team is professionally trained according to the needs. This results in:

less claims

decreasing costs

high customer satisfaction

Monthly protocol

We provide our customers with a monthly protocol of our statistics. Through this protocol we can give a regular feedback to the customer and they can overview the main statements made by the collected data on one paper. It shows the different statistic types, the specifications that were made at the beginning of the work and if they were adhered to. The protocol also shows if any unscheduled changes had happen to influence the statistics and which solutions are planned to adhere the specifications in the future.

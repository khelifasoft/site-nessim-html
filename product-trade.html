Our product trade and delivery

PROFIS INDUSTRIE not only offers services, but is also your reliable partner when it comes to hygiene products. Through many years of experience and contacts with experienced suppliers we offer various hygiene products to be more flexible and effective. Browse our Shop here.

Especially the food industry benefits from our versatile offer such as:

  • Detergents and disinfectants
  • Staff hygiene
  • Cleaning utensils
  • Small appliances
  • etc.

To where do we deliver?

Our headquarter in the Hanseatic City of Lübeck is responsible for the nothern customers.

To be more flexible and to be near to all of our customers, we decided to place the second branch in Karlstein near Frankfurt. This way we can provide our customers placed in the southern regions with better service and consultation.

Furthermore, the opening of another branch office in Berlin is in preparation for summer 2021.
